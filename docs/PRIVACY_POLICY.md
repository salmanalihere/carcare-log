# CarCare Log Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 2, 2026**

CarCare Log is a vehicle record-keeping app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information you can enter into the app, where that information is stored, and what happens when you use exports, backups, notifications, or in-app purchases.

CarCare Log is designed to keep your vehicle records on your device. We do not operate a CarCare Log account system or cloud database for those records.

## 1. Information you choose to store

You can choose to enter or save information such as:

- Vehicle details, including a vehicle name, make, model, year, registration number, VIN or chassis number, engine number, vehicle type and fuel type
- Purchase details, odometer readings and mileage history
- Fuel entries, including dates, quantities, fuel type, station, price and receipts
- Expenses, service history, maintenance records and maintenance schedules
- Insurance and registration details, expiry dates and reminders
- Notes, service-provider details and other information you add to a record
- Vehicle photos, document images and receipt images that you select

Some of this information may identify you or relate to property you own. Please enter only information you need for your own vehicle records and take care when adding sensitive documents.

## 2. Where your information is stored

The information you enter is stored in CarCare Log's app-specific storage on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage technologies.

CarCare Log does not require you to create an account. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your vehicle records, and the app does not send those records to us for analytics, advertising or profiling.

Your iPhone and Apple account settings are separate from CarCare Log. Depending on your iOS settings and Apple's backup services, app data stored on your device may be included in an iPhone backup managed by Apple. That device-level backup is controlled by your Apple settings and Apple's services, not by a CarCare Log cloud-sync service.

## 3. Information we do not request for normal app use

CarCare Log does not need access to your contacts, precise location or Health data for its vehicle-management features. The app does not require an online account.

The app's privacy manifest declares no data types collected by the app for Apple's App Privacy reporting and declares that the app does not track users. CarCare Log does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 4. Photos, receipts and documents

When you choose a photo through Apple's photo picker, CarCare Log receives the selected image so it can be saved to the relevant vehicle, fuel, expense or document record. The app does not need unrestricted access to your entire photo library for this selection flow.

Selected images and attachments are stored with your app records on your device. They are not automatically sent to CarCare Log or to an external document-storage service.

A JSON backup may include vehicle photos, receipt images and document attachments. Treat a backup file as containing potentially sensitive information.

## 5. Reports, exports and backups

CarCare Log can create reports and backup files, including PDF, CSV and JSON formats, depending on the feature you use.

- PDF and CSV reports contain vehicle and financial or maintenance record information included in the selected report.
- A JSON backup can include vehicle details, records, mileage history, schedules, reminders, photos and document attachments.
- Exported files are created when you choose the relevant export or backup action.
- When you share or save a file using the iOS share sheet, Files, email, cloud storage or another destination, the file is sent to the destination you select.

The privacy and security of a destination you choose are governed by that destination's provider. We do not control what happens to a file after you share it outside the app.

Keep exported reports and backups secure. Anyone who obtains an unprotected backup may be able to read the information it contains.

## 6. Importing and restoring backups

You may choose a JSON backup file for import. CarCare Log validates the backup before applying it.

The app offers a merge option and a replace/restore option. Merge adds vehicles not already present in the backup. Replace/restore deletes the current app records and replaces them with the records in the selected backup. The app displays a confirmation because replacement is destructive.

You are responsible for choosing the correct backup file and keeping a separate copy of any information you want to retain before replacing your current records.

## 7. Notifications

CarCare Log can schedule local iOS notifications for reminders such as maintenance, insurance, registration, document expiry and custom reminders.

The app asks for notification permission when you enable or configure reminder features, rather than requiring it to use the app at first launch. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

These reminders are generated on your device. They are not a service appointment, insurance renewal or legal notice, and they may be affected by notification permissions, device settings or other iOS behavior.

## 8. In-app purchases

CarCare Log offers optional Pro features through Apple's in-app purchase system, StoreKit 2. Purchase options may include monthly or yearly subscriptions and a lifetime purchase, as shown in the App Store at the time of purchase.

Apple processes payments and provides the purchase and subscription services. CarCare Log does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. The app checks Apple's StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to determine whether Pro access is active.

Apple's handling of App Store account, payment and transaction information is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. Please review Apple's privacy information for details about information Apple processes.

## 9. When information may leave your device

CarCare Log does not send your vehicle records to our own server. Information may leave your device in the following situations initiated or provided by you or by Apple:

- You export, save or share a report or backup to a destination you choose.
- You contact support by email and include information in your message or attachments.
- Apple processes an App Store purchase, subscription, restore-purchases request or related StoreKit transaction.
- Your device performs a backup using Apple services under your iOS settings.

We do not sell your vehicle records or use them for targeted advertising.

## 10. Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app's local storage until you delete them, clear app data using an available in-app function, or remove the app and its associated data, subject to iOS and device-backup behavior.

Deleting a record or removing the app does not delete copies you previously exported, shared or saved elsewhere. Device backups managed by Apple may also be subject to your Apple account settings and Apple's retention practices.

Before clearing app data, replacing records from a backup or deleting the app, export a backup if you want to keep your information.

## 11. Security

CarCare Log uses Apple's iOS storage and security facilities. You should also protect your iPhone with a passcode or other device security, keep iOS up to date, and store exported files and backups in a secure location.

No app, device or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Children's privacy

CarCare Log is a general vehicle-management app and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account or online service, because the app does not provide a CarCare Log account or vehicle-record server.

## 13. Third-party services

The principal external platform service used for app purchases is Apple's App Store / StoreKit. The app also uses standard iOS capabilities for photo selection, local notifications, file import and export, and sharing.

Those Apple services are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. CarCare Log does not control Apple's services or the services of any destination you select when sharing a file.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
