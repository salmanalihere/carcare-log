# CarCare Log — Privacy Policy (for hosting)

CarCare Log stores everything you enter — vehicles, fuel, expenses, maintenance,
documents and photos — only on your device, in the app's private storage.
The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it
never uploads your data anywhere.

- Purchases are processed by Apple (StoreKit); we never see your payment details.
- Notifications are local notifications generated on your device.
- Backup files are created only when you export them, and go only where you send them.
- Deleting the app deletes its on-device data. "Clear All Data" in Settings does the same in-app.
