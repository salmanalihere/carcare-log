# CarCare Log Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 2, 2026**

CarCare Log ("CarCare Log", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CarCare Log iOS application.

## 1. Information You Enter

CarCare Log allows you to enter and store vehicle-management information, including:

- Vehicle name, make, model, year, registration number, VIN/chassis number, engine number and odometer readings
- Fuel records and fuel-efficiency information
- Expenses, repairs and maintenance records
- Insurance and registration dates
- Notes and service-provider information
- Vehicle photos
- Receipts and vehicle documents you choose to attach

This information is designed to be stored locally on your device. CarCare Log does not require you to create an account to use the app.

## 2. Local Storage

CarCare Log is designed as a local-first application. Your vehicle records, expenses, maintenance records, photos and documents are stored locally on your device using Apple's native storage technologies.

We do not operate a CarCare Log cloud database for your vehicle records.

## 3. Data Collection and Tracking

CarCare Log does not intentionally collect or sell personal information for advertising or tracking purposes.

The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social tracking SDKs, or third-party tracking services for the vehicle data you enter.

## 4. Photos and Documents

If you choose to attach a photo, receipt, insurance document, registration document, warranty document or other file, the app accesses that content only to provide the feature you requested.

Attachments are intended to remain in the app's local storage unless you explicitly export, share or back them up using an iOS feature or destination you choose.

You should avoid storing documents in the app that you are not comfortable keeping on your device or including in an export or backup.

## 5. Notifications

CarCare Log may request permission to send local notifications for features such as maintenance, insurance and registration reminders.

These reminders are generated locally by the app. Notification permission can be controlled through iOS Settings.

## 6. Location, Contacts and Health Data

CarCare Log does not require access to your precise location, contacts, Health data, or other unrelated device information to provide its core vehicle-management features.

## 7. Exports and Backups

CarCare Log may allow you to create PDF, CSV or JSON exports/backups.

When you use iOS sharing, Files, email, cloud storage, messaging, or another destination to export or share information, that information is transferred to the destination you select. The privacy practices of that destination are controlled by its provider, not by CarCare Log.

You are responsible for keeping exported files and backups secure.

## 8. In-App Purchases

CarCare Log uses Apple's StoreKit services for optional CarCare Log Pro purchases.

Available purchase types may include:

- Monthly subscription
- Yearly subscription
- Lifetime purchase

Apple processes payments and manages App Store purchase information. CarCare Log does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Subscription and purchase entitlements are verified using Apple's StoreKit mechanisms.

## 9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your vehicle records or personal information.

CarCare Log does not upload your vehicle records to us for advertising, profiling or sale.

Information may leave the device when you explicitly choose to export or share it, or when Apple processes an App Store purchase or other Apple-provided service.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because vehicle records are primarily stored locally, you can delete them through the app's data-management features or by deleting the app and its associated local data, subject to Apple's device and backup behavior.

Before deleting the app or its data, you should create an export or backup if you want to retain your records.

## 11. Security

CarCare Log uses Apple's native iOS storage and security mechanisms. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, passcode, exported files and backups.

## 12. Children's Privacy

CarCare Log is a general vehicle-management application and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account or advertising system.

## 13. Third-Party Services

CarCare Log relies on Apple-provided platform services, including StoreKit for purchases and iOS notification and sharing capabilities.

Those services are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or legal requirements change. The "Last Updated" date at the top will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
